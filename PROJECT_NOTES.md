## Important interpretation

The screenshot shows a country-level correlation result of approximately 0.0688 between total consumption and average age. This is a very weak linear relationship and should not be presented as evidence of a strong age-consumption relationship.

For a production reliability use case, the recommended next analytical level is WTG/component-level modelling with exposure and operational covariates.
